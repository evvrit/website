# ev/vrit

A personal website about knitting: writing, knitting patterns, and the occasional experiment.

## Language

**Post**:
A piece of writing published on the site.
_Avoid_: Article, essay, blog (the blog is the collection of Posts, not one of them)

**Pattern**:
Knitting instructions for making a specific item. Some Patterns can be customised by the reader, but that isn't what makes something a Pattern.
_Avoid_: Recipe, tutorial

**Published**:
On the site and linked from the home page. Every page is either Published or Retracted.
_Avoid_: Live, listed

**Retracted**:
Withdrawn by choice and no longer reachable on the site, even by people who have the link. A Retracted page is kept privately and can be Published again. Whether to also Expunge it is a separate decision, made for each page.
_Avoid_: Unpublished, hidden, removed

**Expunged**:
A Retracted page whose public record has also been removed from the site's history. The private copy is still kept.
_Avoid_: Purged, scrubbed

**Dropped**:
Removed from the site with no private copy kept. Its history is left alone.
_Avoid_: Deleted, discarded

**Erased**:
Removed from the site, from the site's history, and from private storage, leaving no trace.
_Avoid_: Destroyed, wiped
